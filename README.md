  <h3>A project by Gabriel-Andre Exbrayat, sophomore at the French School of Bali, Louis-Antoine de Bougainville</h3>
  
  <p>This research has for its main objective to relieve the restoration and conservation effort by reducing the manpower needed for monitoring, taking into account all possible budget and material limitations that may affect the populations of the zones that are the most affected (i.e. the coral triangle countries). The research is based on machine learning, and on a BHD dataset. It hasn't been trained enough or put under enough constraints to be considered a ready-to-be-used conventionally system, but on that BHD dataset it reached 97% accuracy, even if I need to consider the possibility of overfitting.</p>
